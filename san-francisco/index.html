How San Francisco Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
