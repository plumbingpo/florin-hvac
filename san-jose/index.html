Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Jose

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
